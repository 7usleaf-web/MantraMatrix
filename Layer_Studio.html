2. Identification & Scope

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Service govern your use of the mobile application Layer Studio (also distributed as Liquid Minimalist Pro), designed for Android devices, operated by [Insert Developer or Organization Name] ("we", "our", or "us").

Layer Studio is a design utility allowing users to design, configure, preview, and apply custom minimalist widgets to their Android home screen. We operate on a strict Privacy-by-Design architecture: we believe your customizations, designs, and devices belong to you alone.

3. Information We Collect

We classify data into two clear categories: local device data and voluntary community transmissions.

A. Information Stored Locally On Your Device (Not Transmitted)

  • Widget Configurations & Drafts: Shape styles, layout dimensions, hex colors, timestamps, custom text tags, and formatting rules.
  • Custom Font Files: When you import custom typography files (.ttf or .otf), they are decoded strictly in-memory or saved inside local client storage. They are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Favorites & Liked Widgets: Your library bookmarks remain strictly on your device.

B. Information Voluntarily Shared via Community Explore

If and only if you choose to publish a widget configuration publicly to the Community Explore feed, the following data is transmitted:

  • Widget Metadata: Widget title, creator display name (freely chosen pseudonym), structural JSON parameters, and creation timestamp.
  • Pseudonymous User Token: A randomly generated client-side identifier (e.g., usr_a8f9c2...) generated via JavaScript. This token contains zero device hardware identifiers and serves exclusively to group your published designs together.

4. Information We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

Zero-Tracking Affirmation:

To eliminate ambiguity for Google Play Developer Program Policy checks, we formally affirm that Layer Studio does NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as real legal names, physical addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Persistent Hardware Identifiers (No IMEI, No MAC Address, No Hardware Serial, No Advertising ID / GAID).
  • No Geolocation Data (neither precise GPS nor coarse network location).
  • No Biometric, Health, Financial, or Payment Data.
  • No Microphone, Camera, Contact Books, SMS, Call Logs, or Installed Applications lists.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer) or Behavioral Tracking.
  • No Targeted Advertising Networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads).

5. How We Use the Information

The limited data handled by the application is utilized solely to deliver user-requested features:

  1. Rendering Widgets: Parsing local widget configuration arrays to render graphics in real-time.
  2. Facilitating Community Sharing: Transmitting submitted designs to public community endpoints so other users can explore and import them.
  3. Spam & Duplicate Prevention: Utilizing the pseudonymous UUID token to moderate submissions and prevent duplicate spam entries.

6. Third-Party Endpoints & Sub-Processors

Layer Studio does not maintain private centralized databases. When community features are accessed, the application communicates securely with the following infrastructure:

  • Google Sheets & Google Apps Script (Google Cloud Platform):
    • Fetching Public Widgets: The app queries a read-only Google Visualization API endpoint (tq?tqx=out:json) to load the community feed.
    • Publishing Widgets: Community submissions are sent via HTTPS POST requests to a secured Google Apps Script web endpoint.
    • Data is processed under Google's standard enterprise privacy and security protocols (Google Privacy Policy).

7. Android Permissions & Native Bridges

Layer Studio requests minimal system permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively to download community widget presets from the Explore feed and upload designs you choose to share. The app works fully offline for local widget creation.
  • JavaScript-to-Native Bridge (window.AndroidApp):
    • saveAndPinWidget: Invokes the native Android widget manager (AppWidgetManager.requestPinAppWidget) to place the widget on your home screen.
    • updateStatusBar: Dynamically updates Android system bar colors to complement your selected preview theme.
  • Local Document Selector / Storage Access: Invoked when you choose custom font files (.ttf, .otf). The file is parsed directly into memory via HTML5 FileReader APIs without granting the app background access to your photo library or personal files.

8. Data Retention, Local Cleanup & Erasure

Local Data Deletion

You maintain autonomous control over all data stored on your device. You can purge all drafts, presets, and preferences at any time by:

  • Navigating to the in-app settings and tapping "Clear All Projects", "Reset Favorites", or "Clear Cache".
  • Clearing the app storage directly via Android Settings: Settings > Apps > Layer Studio > Storage > Clear Data.

Community Content Erasure Protocol

If you published a widget to the Explore feed and wish to have it permanently excised from the public Google Sheets database, email our designated privacy team at [Insert Contact Email] with:

  1. The widget title and display author name.
  2. Your pseudonymous publisher token (accessible from the app's Explore profile settings).

Manual deletion requests are audited and fulfilled within 7 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Layer Studio require creating an account? +
No. Layer Studio requires zero user accounts, email sign-ups, or social logins. You can design, customize, and apply widgets immediately upon installation.
Are my custom imported fonts sent to your servers? +
Absolutely not. Any TTF or OTF font you import is rendered locally inside the app sandbox via memory buffers. They are never transmitted over the internet.
Why does the app need Internet permission? +
Internet access is used strictly to load community widgets inside the "Explore" tab and upload public widget configurations if you choose to share them.
