About Us

Mantra Matrix is a digital chant counter and spiritual arena application designed to help practitioners track their daily meditation counts, cycles, and long-term milestones smoothly.

Data Storage & Protection

We believe in absolute data transparency and user control:

  • Local Storage Only: All your daily chant counts, history logs, and user profile details are stored exclusively inside your local device storage.
  • Zero Cloud Tracking: We do not upload, share, or collect any of your personal chanting activities on external servers.

Device Permissions

Our app utilizes minimal native capabilities strictly required for functionality, such as haptic vibration for bead feedback and audio effects upon finishing a cycle.
